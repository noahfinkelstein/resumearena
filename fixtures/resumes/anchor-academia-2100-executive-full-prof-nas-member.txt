RESEARCH
Interfacial electrochemistry and nanostructured materials for energy storage and conversion: solid-state electrolytes, operando characterization of electrode interfaces, and design rules for fast-ion conductors. Roughly 200 peer-reviewed papers (about 60 in Nature, Science, Nature-family journals, JACS, or PNAS); approximately 60,000 citations; h-index 118 (Google Scholar, 2026). Fourteen issued US patents; two licensed to a battery manufacturer and one forming the basis of a company I co-founded in 2014 (acquired 2020).
